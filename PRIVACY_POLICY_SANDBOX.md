# RetroHQ Sandbox Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 8 October 2026**

## 1. About RetroHQ

RetroHQ is an internal software prototype being developed by the Retrograde team to help collectible resellers manage inventory, pricing, listings and sales.

This privacy policy applies to our development and testing activities using eBay's Sandbox environment. RetroHQ is not currently a publicly available commercial service.

**Privacy contact:** jamie@lepetiteparisien.com

## 2. Information We Process

During testing, RetroHQ may process:

- eBay Sandbox account identifiers and authorisation information.
- Test inventory, product descriptions, prices and listings.
- Simulated orders, fulfilment information, cancellations and returns.
- Technical information required to diagnose integration errors.

We do not require real customer transactions to test the Sandbox integration.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use this information to develop and test RetroHQ, connect authorised eBay Sandbox seller accounts, create and manage test listings, simulate sales and returns, and improve system reliability.

Our basis for processing is our legitimate interest in developing and testing the prototype, subject to applicable data protection requirements. Access to eBay account information also requires the account holder's authorisation.

## 4. Security and Storage

API credentials and authorisation tokens are treated as confidential and must not be published in source code.

The current prototype uses temporary application data and development infrastructure. It is not intended for storing real customer information or production transactions.

Test information is retained only as needed for development, troubleshooting and verification, and may be deleted when no longer required.

## 5. Third-Party Services

RetroHQ uses development infrastructure and may connect to eBay Sandbox, GitHub Codespaces and market-data providers.

Information shared with these services is limited to what is needed for the relevant testing or integration function. Their own privacy policies may also apply, including where information is processed outside the United Kingdom.

We do not sell personal information.

## 6. Your Rights

Where applicable under UK data protection law, individuals may request access to, correction of or deletion of their personal information, or object to certain processing.

Requests can be sent to **jamie@lepetiteparisien.com**.

Individuals may also raise concerns with the UK Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk.

## 7. Changes

This policy may be updated as RetroHQ develops. A separate, more comprehensive privacy policy will be prepared before any public commercial launch involving real users or customer information.
