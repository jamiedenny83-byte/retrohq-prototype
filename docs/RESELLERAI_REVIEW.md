# ResellerAI workflow review — 2026-10-15

Source: https://resellerai.org/ (public homepage; no account or live publishing test).

The service advertises photo upload → generated title, description, specifics and price → seller review and eBay publication. It claims sold-comparable pricing with three price levels, photo flaw detection, bulk generation and direct eBay publishing. Its advertised speed and valuation quality are not independently verified here.

## Proposed RetroHQ direction

Use a single review screen after identification, reusing confirmed identity and photos rather than restarting text search. Offer Buy / Save to stock / Prepare listing according to the task. Show suggested price and editable condition, completeness and cost prominently. Ask for extra information only when missing or ambiguous. Keep purchase evidence, preparation costs, trade credit, basket checkout and physical stock history connected to that record.

RetroHQ already implements core buying/inventory features and real photo identification, but does not yet publish listings. Its UK CeX evidence and explicit cost history give it a different focus; superiority in speed or accuracy has not been measured. Active asking prices must remain labelled separately from sold evidence. Photograph-based flaw suggestions need seller review, and functional testing cannot be inferred from appearance.

Priorities for a future approved listing milestone: prefilled local listing drafts, one review screen, required category/shipping/account defaults, then verified eBay publication and reconciliation. Crosslisting needs stock reservations and confirmed delisting outcomes. Do not let listing work block the current usable inventory trial. None of these future marketplace writes are enabled by the current UI change.
